# Third-party materials

The game was imported from the supplied V17.7 HTML. Existing audio and imagery were extracted without altering their bytes. The asset manifest identifies every extracted file by SHA-256; the baseline hash identifies the exact imported artifact.

## Three.js

The embedded Three.js r128 library retains its original code and license header. Three.js is distributed under the MIT License: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r128/LICENSE

## New V17.7 firearm effects

The Free Firearm Sound Library, released as CC0 on OpenGameArt:
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Edits: first-shot extraction, mono 24 kHz PCM, fade and synthesized bass. These designed effects use surrogate firearm recordings, not authenticated recordings of the experimental guns.

## Inherited materials

This import retains the music and effects embedded in the supplied game. Their existing credits and links remain in the game source. CC0 for the new V17.7 sound library does not describe the licensing of every inherited asset.
